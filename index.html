All-in-One E-Commerce Label Processing & Dispatch Management

📑

Multi-Portal Hub

Switch between Amazon, Meesho, and Flipkart workspaces on one single URL. No need to keep separate tabs or bookmark multiple tools.

✂️

Flipkart Invoice Cropper

Crops the top shipping sticker from Flipkart/Shopsy A4 PDF sheets, removing the invoice part automatically for thermal sticker rolls.

📊

SKU Dispatch Summary

Parses seller SKUs and item quantities from invoice & label sheets, giving your warehouse team a real-time pick list.

🖨️

Direct Thermal Printing

Print shipping labels directly to thermal printers (Zebra, Xprinter, TSC) with a single click—no file downloads required.
