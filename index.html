Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I separate Amazon shipping labels from tax invoices?

Simply upload your combined Amazon Easy Ship or ATS bulk PDF file to our online tool. It automatically identifies tax invoice sheets using text matching and extracts only the shipping labels into a clean PDF file.

Is my customer data and sales invoice safe on this website?

Yes, 100%! All processing happens locally in your web browser using WebAssembly and PDF.js. No files, customer names, or invoice data are ever transmitted to any remote server.

Can I print shipping labels directly to my thermal sticker printer?

Yes! Click the Print Labels button next to Download to open your browser's native print dialog directly configured for thermal printers like Zebra, TSC, Xprinter, or Gprinter.

How does the SKU-wise summary report work?

The tool reads the Seller SKU and product quantity from each invoice sheet before filtering it out, providing an aggregated order report so warehouse staff can quickly pick and pack items.
