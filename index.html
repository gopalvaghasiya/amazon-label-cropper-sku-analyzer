How to Crop Amazon Shipping Labels & Separate Tax Invoices for 4x6 Thermal Printers

Amazon generates combined PDF files with shipping labels and full-page tax invoices. Learn how to isolate labels, remove tax invoices, and print barcode stickers on 4x6 thermal paper without wasting adhesive sheets.

Why Do Amazon PDFs Include Tax Invoices?

When you download shipping labels from Amazon Seller Central under Easy Ship or Self Ship, Amazon bundles each customer's 4x6 shipping label alongside a full A4 tax invoice page. If you send the raw PDF directly to a 4x6 thermal roll printer, your printer will attempt to shrink the entire A4 invoice into a 4x6 sticker, resulting in unreadable barcodes and wasted expensive thermal rolls.

Step-by-Step Label Cropping Workflow:

  1. Download PDF: Download your daily dispatch batch from Amazon Seller Central (Manage Orders → Print Shipping Labels).
  2. Upload to Hub: Drag & drop your PDF file into our .
  3. Automatic Filtering: Our client-side algorithm instantly scans each page, identifies pure shipping labels with barcodes, and discards invoice-only pages.
  4. Adjust Cutoff Height: If needed, use the cutoff height slider (default 100%) to trim unnecessary footer margins.
  5. 1-Click Thermal Print: Click "Print Labels" to print directly to your Zebra, Xprinter, or TVS thermal printer with zero dialog resizing.
💡 Pro Tip: Use the SKU Dispatch Report table on the right side to pick items in bulk before packing. This reduces packing station walking time by up to 60%.

Flipkart & Shopsy A4 PDF Cropping: How to Remove the Invoice Half Automatically

Flipkart generates 2-in-1 A4 sheets with the shipping sticker on top and the customer invoice on the bottom. Discover how to isolate the top half into a crisp 4x6 label without using manual scissors.

The Flipkart 2-in-1 Label Problem

Flipkart Seller Hub and Shopsy generate dispatch labels formatted on standard A4 pages. The top half contains the courier routing barcode (Ekart, Shadowfax, Delhivery), while the bottom half contains the tax invoice. Sellers printing on thermal roll printers struggle because printing the full A4 page shrinks the barcode or requires manual cutting.

The Automated Cropping Solution:

  1. Upload Flipkart PDF: Upload single or batch Flipkart invoices into our .

  3. Vector Fidelity: Labels are cropped mathematically using PDF vector operations—never converted to blurry low-res bitmap images.
  4. Direct Print / Download: Print directly or download a clean batch PDF containing only ready-to-stick 4x6 labels.
💡 Ekart Compliance: Cropped labels maintain 100% sharp vector lines, ensuring 0% barcode rejection rates at the Ekart hub scan point.
Hardware & Setup 📖 5 min read

Master Guide: Thermal Printer Setup (Zebra, Xprinter, TSC, TVS, Rollo)

Learn the optimal driver configurations, paper size settings (4x6 inches / 100x150mm), and browser print parameters for crisp, scannable e-commerce shipping labels.

Essential Thermal Printer Driver Configuration:

  • Paper Size: Select 4.00 x 6.00 in (or 100mm x 150mm / 102mm x 152mm). Do NOT leave it on Default A4.
  • Print Speed: Set between 3.0 ips and 5.0 ips. Higher speeds can create fuzzy 1D barcode lines.
  • Darkness / Density: Set to 8 - 12 (or 60% - 70%). Avoid maximum darkness as it bleeds ink and connects thin barcode stripes.
  • Dithering / Halftone: Set to None or Error Diffusion for clean monochrome barcodes.

Browser Print Dialog Settings (Chrome / Edge):

  • Destination: Select your thermal printer (e.g. 4BARCODE 4B-2054TF, Xprinter XP-420B, Zebra ZD220).
  • Paper Size: 4x6 in / 100x150mm.
  • Margins: Set to None.
  • Scale: Set to Fit to Printable Area or 100%.
  • Headers and Footers: Uncheck (prevents date/URL printing on stickers).

How SKU-Based Batch Picking Reduces Warehouse Packing Errors & Cuts RTO

Discover the Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) used by high-volume marketplace sellers to pack 500+ orders per hour with zero mismatch returns.

The Cost of Warehouse Dispatch Errors

Shipping the wrong color, size, or variant costs Indian e-commerce sellers between ₹80 to ₹180 in non-refundable forward + reverse logistics fees per return. Over 70% of packing mistakes happen when pickers try to pack one order at a time directly from raw multi-page PDF slips.

The 4-Step Zero-Error Packing SOP:

  1. Step 1 - Batch Pick List: Upload all orders into the tool and print or view the SKU summary table. Bring all items from the inventory racks in a single bulk round.
  2. Step 2 - SKU Sorting: Organize items on the packing table grouped by SKU.
  3. Step 3 - Sequential Packing: Because cropped labels are sorted in matching sequence, the packer attaches each 4x6 sticker directly onto the pre-sorted parcel.
  4. Step 4 - Scan Verification: Perform a rapid handheld barcode scan check against the daily dispatch manifest before pickup handover.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

🔒 Is my customer invoice data safe when uploading to this tool?
Yes, 100%! All PDF parsing, invoice filtering, and label cropping happens entirely inside your web browser via client-side JavaScript (pdf-lib & PDF.js). No PDF files, customer details, addresses, or prices are ever uploaded to any server or third party.
🖨️ Which thermal printers are supported?
All standard 4-inch (4x6 in / 100x150mm) direct thermal and thermal transfer barcode printers are supported, including Zebra, Xprinter, TSC, TVS, Dymo, Rollo, HPRT, Everycom, and Godex.
⚡ Can I print shipping labels directly without downloading a PDF file?
Yes! Simply click the "Print Labels" button in any portal workspace. An optimized 4x6 print frame will open immediately, allowing you to print straight to your thermal printer with zero disk clutter.
💰 Is this multi-marketplace tool completely free to use?
Yes! This utility is 100% free and open-source for all e-commerce sellers, warehouse managers, and logistics teams. There are no subscriptions, limits, or hidden fees.
